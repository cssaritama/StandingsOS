# Product behavior specification

This file records user-visible behavior that should remain stable unless a deliberate product change is made.

## Competitions and seasons

- A competition has a unique name.
- A competition can contain multiple seasons.
- A season belongs to exactly one competition.
- A season status is `planned`, `active` or `completed`.

## Teams

- Team names are globally unique in this reference implementation.
- A team can participate in multiple seasons.
- A team must be enrolled in a season before it can appear in a fixture for that season.

## Fixtures

- A fixture belongs to one season.
- Home and away teams must be different.
- Both teams must be enrolled in the fixture's season.
- A fixture represents schedule/operational state and may exist with no result.

## Results

- A fixture can have at most one active result.
- Results contain home goals, away goals, source and recorded timestamp.
- Results are not edited in place through the API.
- To correct a result, remove the active result and record a replacement.

## Standings

- Standings are never persisted as independent state.
- Only fixtures with recorded results contribute to standings.
- Win = 3 points, draw = 1, loss = 0.
- Display ordering is points, goal difference, goals for, team name.

## Analytics

Analytics use only recorded results from the selected season.

## Forecasts

- Forecasts are generated only for fixtures without results.
- `elo-v1` uses only results recorded within the selected season.
- Forecast output is presented as a transparent baseline, not a betting recommendation.

## AI brief

- AI is optional and disabled by default.
- The LLM receives a bounded evidence payload generated by deterministic code.
- The AI does not calculate or persist standings, results or forecasts.

## CSV

- Import requires `scheduled_at`, `home_team`, `away_team`.
- Result fields are optional.
- Unknown team names are created and enrolled during import.
- Export emits the same core columns plus result source.
